Big-cap breadth (top-150 coins on Binance)

% of the top-150 market-cap coins (Binance USDT pairs, no stablecoins/wrapped) closing above their 200-day and 50-day averages, daily UTC closes. Dotted lines at 80 and 20 mark the extreme zones. Same method as the 'Breadth crypto' app, recomputed by us from Binance data.

Trench Index (tehcscreener)

Daily 0–100 trench score from tehcscreener.com (/api/v1/trench), same as the 'Trench Index' app: launchpad volume 35%, bot volume 25%, new tokens 20%, bot fees 20%; each a percentile vs the last 90 and 365 days (blended 60/40); rises count the same day, drops ease in (cooldown 0.65). Bands: Ded 0–19, Cold 20–39, Active 40–59, Hot 60–79, Euphoric 80–100. Dotted grey = our recompute from public per-pad/per-bot data (proxy). Orange (right axis) = our PvE/PvP score, for comparison.
